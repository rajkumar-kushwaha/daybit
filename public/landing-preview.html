FAQ

The frequent questions

Everything you need to know about Daybit.

Is Daybit completely free to use?
Yes, 100% free. There are no paywalls, hidden subscriptions, or annoying popups. You get unlimited habits, full monthly views, streak tracking, and offline support completely free.
Does Daybit work offline without internet?
Yes! Daybit is built with local-first technology. It runs smoothly on airplanes, subways, or anywhere with zero signal. All your progress is stored directly on your device.
What is the "Accountability Lock"?
The Accountability Lock lets you seal past dates so you can't go back and check off days you missed. It removes the temptation to fake your streaks and keeps you focused on showing up today.
Is my personal habit data private?
Completely. We do not sell your data or track your personal life. Your habits stay on your device. If you optionally choose to sign in with Google or Email, your data is securely synced to your private encrypted cloud backup.
How do I install Daybit on my phone?
Daybit is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On iPhone, open Daybit in Safari, tap the Share button, and select "Add to Home Screen". On Android, open in Chrome and tap "Install App". It opens in full-screen just like a native app.
Can I rearrange or reorder my habits?
Yes! Open the three dots menu (⋮) in the top bar, tap "Reorder Habits", and you can hold and drag any habit up or down with your finger to arrange them however fits your morning or evening routine best.
